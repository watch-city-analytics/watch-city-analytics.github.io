About

An applied AI consultancy built around a single conviction about why most AI projects stall.

The problem we exist to solve

Access to capable AI models is no longer a differentiator. Nearly every business can reach the same models on the same terms. What separates the organizations getting real value from the ones stuck in permanent pilot is not the technology — it is whether anyone involved genuinely understood the work being automated.

This is the gap we were built to close. We bring real-world knowledge of how specific industries operate, and we apply AI to those workflows with that understanding as the starting point rather than a consideration added late.

How we work

We are deliberately model-agnostic. Open source and commercial models each have workloads they suit, and the right architecture frequently uses both. Choosing between them is an engineering decision driven by the problem, the data, and the economics — not by a vendor relationship.

We are also deliberately narrow. Rather than claiming competence across every sector, we build depth in the verticals we know, and we recruit specialists who bring that depth with them. The consultants we work with have operated inside the industries they advise on.

We would rather tell a client that a workflow is not ready for AI than deliver something that looks impressive and quietly fails in production. That judgment is most of what we are actually selling.

The name

Watch City is Waltham, Massachusetts — the mill town that built American watchmaking by bringing precision manufacturing to something that had been done by hand, one craftsman at a time. The parallel is not subtle, and we did not choose it by accident.
