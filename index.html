Applied AI Consulting

AI that understands the work it is being asked to do.

We build verticalized AI solutions for real business workflows — using open source and commercial models together, and grounding both in genuine knowledge of the industry they are deployed into.

What we do Start a conversation

Everyone agrees AI needs customization. Very few actually do it.

The general-purpose model is the easy part. The hard part is the distance between a capable model and a workflow that a specific business actually runs on — the exceptions, the vocabulary, the handoffs, the regulatory edges, and the reasons things are done the way they are done.

That distance is closed by people who understand the vertical, not by people who only understand the model. It is the work we do.

01

Vertical first

We start from how the industry operates, not from what the technology can demo. Domain expertise is the input, not an afterthought bolted on at the end.

02

Open source and commercial

We are not tied to a single vendor or model. Some work belongs on a commercial frontier model; some belongs on an open-weight model you control. We choose based on the workload, the data, and the cost.

03

Into the workflow

A pilot that never reaches production is a cost, not a capability. We build toward the systems and processes people already use every day.

How we engage

Most engagements begin small and deliberately narrow — one workflow, assessed honestly, including whether AI is the right answer for it at all.

Assessment

Map the workflow as it actually runs. Identify where AI changes the economics and where it does not.

Implementation

Build the solution against your data, your systems, and your constraints — not a reference architecture.

Optimization

Measure what shipped, improve what underperforms, and retire what is not earning its keep.
