Enterprise expertise, pointed somewhere it has never been

Large companies have architects. People whose whole job is to ask what happens to the data, what this costs in year three, and what it takes to leave a vendor. Everyone else gets sold to as though they have those people, and they do not.

We bring that bench. Our work pairs industry veterans — people who ran operations, finance, athletics and property before they ever worked in AI — with architects who have spent decades designing data systems at enterprise scale. Our founder has spent more than thirty years building IT systems, nearly three decades of it at EMC and Dell, doing one thing over and over: working out what a business actually needs and then deciding what should be built for it. That is a long time to learn how organizations get trapped by their own tools.

What you are buying is access. People who have run operations like yours, sitting beside architects who have built the systems that hold the records of companies with a hundred thousand employees. On your problem from the first week, without you paying for anyone to learn your business first.

It started with a town that could not afford to compete

A few years ago the question was narrow and practical: how does a community that cannot afford the dominant video platform give its kids the same shot as the town down the road?

At the time, it could not. Film study and opponent breakdown sat behind a subscription some programs could absorb and others could not, and the gap compounded every season. Then it became clear what was possible once AI was applied to the problem — not as a product to sell, but as a way to rebuild the capability out of parts a town could actually afford.

That is the whole firm. Build what a small organization is being priced out of, on infrastructure it controls.

Three things we believe that most firms do not

Your data is the advantage

If a platform learns from your operation and sells that improvement to everyone in your category, you have funded your competitor's capability. We build so what you know stays yours.

We design to what you have

There are a lot of these AI systems now, and they are not all alike. We pick the one that suits the job and your budget — including, very often, the one you already pay for and barely use. We will not send you out to buy what everyone else bought.

Leaving should be easy

What we build runs on your accounts, in your storage, documented well enough that you could replace us. Anything else is a trap with a nicer name.

Start by telling us about one job

Ten minutes, online, in your own words. We ask the same questions we would ask sitting in your office — all of them about how the work gets done, none of them about technology. At the end you get the job written down properly, start to finish. That is yours to keep whether you ever call us or not.
