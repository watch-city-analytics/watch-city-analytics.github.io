Services

Three engagements that build on each other. Most clients start with the first and decide from there.

AI Workflow Assessment

Where to start

We map a workflow the way it actually runs — including the exceptions and workarounds that never appear in the process documentation — and identify where AI meaningfully changes the cost, speed, or quality of the work.

The deliverable is a clear-eyed view of which parts are worth automating, which parts should stay with people, and what it would take to do either. That includes telling you when the answer is that AI is not the right tool for this problem yet.

Custom Implementation

Building it

We build against your systems, your data, and your constraints. Model selection is a design decision, not a default: open-weight models where control, cost, or data residency matter, commercial frontier models where capability does, and often both in the same solution.

The target is always production. Integrations into the tools your team already uses, evaluation of whether outputs hold up on real cases, and handover documentation written for the people who will live with the system.

Ongoing Optimization

Keeping it working

AI systems drift. Models change underneath you, usage patterns shift, and the workflow the solution was built for evolves. We measure what shipped against the outcomes it was meant to produce and keep it aligned as both move.

That also means being willing to retire what is not earning its keep, rather than defending it because it was built.

How we are different

A great deal of AI consulting is model expertise in search of a problem. We work the other way around: the industry knowledge comes first, and the technology is selected to serve it.

That is why we focus on specific verticals rather than claiming to serve every sector equally well. Depth is the product.
